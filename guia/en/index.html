Gemini 4 in practice

Gemini 4 in practice is a comparison for people who use AI for tasks that need to work in the real world. It brings together Google’s sources, a snapshot of the Agent Arena, and a simple repetition lab. You can read the comparison now and use the spreadsheet whenever you want to test it with your own work.

2 p.m. · Deliver a page

One task, the same yardstick

Give all three models the same data, tools, and attempt limit. Decide in advance what counts as done. Add up the cost of all attempts and divide only by the number of deliverables that passed.

What the Arena observed

In the snapshot reviewed, Neon had the highest score of the three. Hydrogen had the lowest median time. Argon had the lowest median cost. These rankings describe that snapshot. INEMA conducted desk research and prepared a protocol for future in-house tests.

Test with something you need to deliver

Choose a lab task, copy the prompt, and use the spreadsheet to record the run. Start with fictional data.

Can I get started without paying?

Hydrogen offers a free tier with limits in the Gemini API. The prices shown in the comparison correspond to the standard paid tier. Availability in the app, AI Studio, and Antigravity depends on the product surface and plan.
